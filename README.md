# Breaker's Yard

A first-person night-time chase in a scrapyard, played in the browser on a phone or a computer.

You came over the fence for parts. At midnight the gate locked itself, and the man who runs the yard is still here. Pull three fuses from the junction boxes, wire them into the gate panel, and get out before he finds you.

**Play:** https://drfillmore.github.io/Blackwood-Lodge/

## How to play

| | Phone | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Walk / sidestep | Put your left thumb down anywhere on the left | W A S D |
| Look around | Drag on the right side of the screen | Mouse (click the game first); arrow keys turn |
| Sprint | Push the left stick all the way up (it glows yellow) | Shift |
| Pull fuse, hide, wire the gate | Yellow action button | E |
| Torch on/off | TORCH | F |

- He sees what his headlamp sees. Floodlights and your torch let him see you from further away.
- Climb into an open wreck to hide. He won't find you unless he watched you get in.
- Pulling a fuse makes noise, kills a floodlight and makes him faster.

## Comfort settings

If moving around makes you queasy, tap **Comfort** (top right of the title screen, or under the breath bar during play). You can switch off head bob, screen shake and sprint zoom. The game pauses while the panel is open, and your choices are remembered on that device.
